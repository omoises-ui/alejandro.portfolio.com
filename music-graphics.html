Client 01

Skullcandy

Music Culture · Copywriting · Audience Insight · Premium Audio
Skullcandy Latin America

Developed directly with Skullcandy Latin America, this campaign set out to reconnect the brand with an adult audience of music lovers — particularly people aged 35 and over.

This segment represented an attractive audience with purchasing power, a strong interest in technology and a deep emotional connection to music. However, the brand felt it was gradually losing relevance with them as most of its communication had increasingly focused on a much younger target.

The launch of a new line of premium noise-cancelling headphones became an opportunity to regain their attention and speak to them through a language they immediately understood: the music they had grown up with and still loved.

There was one important limitation: there was almost no production budget.

So we solved the problem the old-fashioned way — with one of advertising’s most enduring tools: a strong idea expressed through a strong headline.

The Insight

As this audience gets older, their relationship with music often becomes more demanding, not less.

Many of them evolve from casual listeners into true music enthusiasts: people who know what they like, care about sound quality and are willing to invest in better technology because they can afford it.

They want to hear the music they love with the best possible quality — and they do not want anything interfering with that experience.

That led us to a simple insight: The more you care about music, the less willing you are to let anything interrupt it.

The Idea

We created a headline-driven graphic campaign built around iconic music references.

Instead of explaining noise cancellation through technical specifications, we dramatized what happens when everyday noise gets between you and the music you love.

We turned unwanted sounds into unexpected members of familiar bands.

Each execution altered a recognizable band name and visual identity by introducing the very noises the product was designed to eliminate.

The humor came from the collision between something the audience knew intimately and an interruption they immediately understood.

The message was simple: Don’t let noise affect your music.

My Role

I worked on the project as Creative Director, Copywriter & Project Manager, also handling the account relationship directly with the client.

My role included developing the creative concept, identifying the musical and cultural references behind the campaign, shaping the headlines and copy, and helping direct the overall creative execution.

In parallel, I managed the project directly with Skullcandy Latin America, coordinating communication, feedback and the development of the campaign from the client side through execution.

The work connected Skullcandy’s premium noise-cancelling benefit with the audience’s increasingly demanding relationship with music and sound quality.

How I Think

Find a real audience truth, connect it with culture, and express it simply enough that the idea can work even when the budget cannot.

Impact

With almost no production budget, the campaign transformed a limitation into part of the creative strategy.

By relying on music culture, audience insight, copywriting and a simple graphic system instead of expensive production, the work created a series of executions designed to reconnect Skullcandy with an older, more demanding music audience while communicating the benefit of premium noise cancellation in a direct and memorable way.
