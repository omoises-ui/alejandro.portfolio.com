Before moving into advertising, creative direction and music project development, I worked in broadcast journalism. The work demanded speed, judgement and the ability to turn information into stories people could understand.

My experience included national television reporting and production work for radio programs led by well-known Venezuelan broadcasters. The archive is limited, but the work shaped the editorial instincts that later became central to my creative career.
