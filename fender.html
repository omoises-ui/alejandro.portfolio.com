Kurt Cobain Jaguar

Turning an iconic artist’s cultural legacy into a single, provocative outdoor headline for the Fender Kurt Cobain Signature Jaguar.

Project
Client: Fender Latin America
Year: 2019
Market: Latin America
Media: Outdoor Advertising
Product: Kurt Cobain Signature Jaguar
Project: Freelance — developed directly with the client

In 2019, Fender Latin America approached us with a straightforward brief: create an outdoor campaign that would put the product at the center of the communication and promote the Kurt Cobain Signature Jaguar across Latin America.

The conditions made the challenge more interesting.

There was virtually no production budget, the campaign had to be developed in just three days, and outdoor media demanded an idea that could be understood almost instantly.

The solution was reduced to its most essential elements: an iconic product, a powerful cultural truth and one short, memorable headline.

Campaign in Context

Outdoor Advertising

The campaign was conceived specifically for outdoor media, where a complete story needed to be understood in only a few seconds.

He wanted to die. But he couldn’t.

The Insight

Kurt Cobain’s physical absence is one of the most tragic and widely remembered parts of his story.

Yet decades after his death, he never truly disappeared.

His music, image, influence and artistic legacy remain deeply embedded in popular culture and continue to influence new generations of musicians and listeners.

That contradiction became the starting point for the campaign: Cobain may have chosen to disappear physically, but culturally, he never succeeded in disappearing at all.

The Fender Jaguar associated with him had become one of the objects through which that legacy continued to exist.

The Idea

Outdoor advertising gives you only a few seconds.

There was no space for a long story, and no budget to manufacture one through an elaborate production.

So we compressed the entire narrative into a single provocative headline: He wanted to die. But he couldn’t.

The line was paired directly with the Kurt Cobain Signature Jaguar, making the guitar both the product and the resolution of the story.

In only a few words, the execution connected Cobain’s physical absence with the persistence of his artistic influence.

The product became evidence of the idea: an artist can disappear, but what he creates can remain.

Rather than simply showing another signature guitar, the campaign used the cultural story behind the instrument to give the product meaning.

My Role

For this release, my hands-on role was primarily as Copywriter.

I worked on translating the strategic and cultural insight surrounding Kurt Cobain into a headline capable of functioning in outdoor media, where the message needed to be immediate, memorable and inseparable from the product.

The challenge was to tell a much larger story — about absence, legacy and cultural permanence — using almost no words.

The creative direction was developed together with David Carrillo, who also led the art direction of the execution.

How I Think

When you only have a few seconds to tell a story, find the cultural truth that can make a few words carry the weight of an entire narrative.

Impact

With virtually no production budget and only three days to develop the work, the project reduced the campaign to the elements that mattered most: the product, the cultural insight and the headline.

The result was a single outdoor execution designed to turn the Kurt Cobain Signature Jaguar from a product image into the final piece of a larger story about artistic legacy.

Rather than competing for attention through production value, the campaign relied on an idea that could be understood in seconds — an essential requirement for outdoor advertising.
