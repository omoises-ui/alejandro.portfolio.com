Case 03

El éxito es que lo estás intentando

Creative Direction · Copywriting · Storytelling · Brand Strategy

Developed at Wiper Agency for the Argentine market, this campaign for Acindar set out to launch a new business division designed to facilitate access to credit for small and medium-sized enterprises.

The challenge was significant: how could we motivate entrepreneurs in the middle of a financial crisis, with high interest rates, inflation and an environment in which conventional messages about business growth felt increasingly disconnected from reality?

Rather than speaking about success in the usual aspirational way, we decided to question the concept itself and rebuild it from a more human and relevant perspective.

The Insight

“Success” is one of the most repeated ideas in the business world, but it is often represented as something distant, external and almost unattainable.

In a context where entrepreneurs were facing uncertainty, financial pressure and difficult conditions, that traditional definition no longer felt relevant.

The real insight was that, for many people, success was not necessarily reaching the finish line. Sometimes, success was simply having the courage to keep trying despite the odds.

The Idea

That observation became the foundation of El éxito es que lo estás intentando.

We redefined success from an external measure of achievement into a personal feeling of pride, courage and resilience.

Instead of presenting success as the unreachable carrot everyone is expected to chase, the campaign celebrated those who continue moving forward even when circumstances are against them.

It’s not about what other people’s success looks like. It’s about what your success looks like.

Through storytelling, the campaign gave Acindar a more human and emotionally relevant role in the entrepreneurial ecosystem.

My Role

I worked on the project as Creative Director & Creative Copywriter.

My role included developing the strategic and creative direction of the campaign, shaping the central storytelling concept, defining the tone and narrative, and writing the creative content across the different campaign pieces.

How I Think

Sometimes the strongest idea does not come from creating a new insight, but from challenging an old one and redefining it for the reality people are actually living.

Impact

The campaign generated strong media visibility and helped create a new public perception around Acindar’s role in supporting small and medium-sized enterprises.

By moving away from a conventional financial message and focusing instead on the emotional reality of entrepreneurs, the campaign positioned the new business division within a broader narrative of resilience, courage and support for those trying to move their businesses forward.
