Different Industries.
One A&R Profile.

My career spans music, journalism, advertising, creative leadership and entrepreneurship. Seen separately, these are different chapters. Seen through A&R, they form one profile: taste, research, storytelling, artist-side experience, audience thinking, project leadership and commercial awareness.

What connects the journey

I have repeatedly worked at the point where ideas, people and audiences need to connect. Journalism trained my judgement. Advertising trained my positioning and communication. Entrepreneurship trained my commercial discipline. Independent music projects trained me to develop artists from the inside. A&R is where those capabilities now converge.
