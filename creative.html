Case 01

Missing Children Argentina

Creative Concept · Technology Insight · Creative Direction
Missing Children is one of Argentina’s leading NGOs dedicated to finding missing children. The challenge was not simply to increase awareness, but to make every published search more effective.

The Insight

The starting point came from two observations. First, the emergence of Facebook’s DeepFace facial-recognition technology opened a new technological possibility. Second, there was a simple human truth: no matter how many times people see the photograph of a missing child, they are unlikely to accurately remember that child’s face when encountering them later in real life.

The Idea

That intersection between technology and human behavior became the foundation of the idea. We transformed Facebook’s facial-recognition capabilities into a new tool for searching for missing children, expanding the potential reach and effectiveness of each case beyond the limitations of a traditional awareness campaign.

My Role

My involvement began at the origin of the concept: identifying the potential of facial-recognition technology and connecting it with the behavioral insight behind the problem. From there, I helped conceptualize how an existing technology could be reinterpreted creatively and turned into a meaningful solution for the organization.

How I Think

Seeing potential in something that already exists, making an unexpected connection, and giving that connection a clear purpose.

Impact

The campaign became one of Missing Children Argentina’s most recognized initiatives and received multiple international advertising awards, including recognition at the Cannes Lions International Festival of Creativity.
