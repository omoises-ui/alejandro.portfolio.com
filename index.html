A&R · Artist Development · Creative Direction · Music Strategy

I come to A&R from the inside of artist development. I have written songs, built independent music projects, shaped identities, led creative teams, developed live formats, generated media attention and grown audiences across different markets. That experience is reinforced by a career in journalism, advertising and entrepreneurship — giving me the mix of taste, editorial judgement, audience understanding and execution that modern A&R requires.

Now based in Berlin, I am focused on identifying distinctive artists, understanding why a song or project can matter culturally, and helping turn potential into a coherent artistic and commercial path.

Creator experience + editorial judgement + creative strategy + audience thinking + hands-on project development.
About me · Music, culture & curiosity

I listen to music. And to what society says through it.

Music has accompanied me throughout my life. I love living with it, but I also love understanding it: as emotion, as culture and as a conversation through which societies speak to themselves. I believe we cannot fully understand humanity without listening to its music.

I bring a journalist’s curiosity and a constant appetite for musical information: emerging scenes, artists shaping the present, audience behaviour and the social context around a song. I want to understand why something connects now — and what that might tell us about the next step.

How I Think as A&R

Data can show movement. Judgement decides what is worth moving with.
01Spot Potential
Artist, song, timing.Evaluate artistic identity, repertoire, cultural relevance and the signals that suggest room to grow.
02Develop Artists
From promise to proposition.Help define repertoire, positioning, visual language, storytelling, live identity and next-step priorities.
03Read Scenes
Culture before consensus.Track emerging scenes, movements, communities and behaviours across Berlin, Germany and Europe.
04Evaluate Repertoire
Songs first.Use creator experience to assess songwriting, distinctiveness, emotional impact, production direction and catalogue potential.
05Understand Audiences
People behind the numbers.Combine community behaviour, cultural context and performance signals to understand where real connection is forming.

Signal, not substitute.Read streaming, social and growth KPIs without allowing metrics to replace artistic judgement.

08Connect Teams
One artist, one direction.Align artists, management, marketing, PR, distribution, production, creative and live teams around the same development priorities.

Creative reference · Rick Rubin

Instinct. Listening. The essence.

A multi-genre producer who barely plays instruments.

Rick Rubin is a defining reference for how I think about creative and artistic direction. What I admire is his visceral, intuitive approach: listening deeply, recognizing what matters and helping each project find its own identity.

For me, his genius lies in the direction he gives a project beyond technical execution — the sensitivity to know what to protect, what to question and what to strip away. That is the creative leadership I aspire to bring to artists, music and every project I build.

Find the essence. Let it lead.
