A&R · Artist Development · Creative Direction · Music Strategy

I come to A&R from the inside of artist development. I have written songs, built independent music projects, shaped identities, led creative teams, developed live formats, generated media attention and grown audiences across different markets. That experience is reinforced by a career in journalism, advertising and entrepreneurship — giving me the mix of taste, editorial judgement, audience understanding and execution that modern A&R requires.

Now based in Berlin, I am focused on identifying distinctive artists, understanding why a song or project can matter culturally, and helping turn potential into a coherent artistic and commercial path.

Creator experience + editorial judgement + creative strategy + audience thinking + hands-on project development.
