How I Think

I am interested in the space where instinct meets strategy: finding what makes an artist, idea or project distinctive, giving it a clear identity and building the conditions for people to care about it.

About

I started my career in journalism and later moved into advertising, creative direction, strategy and entrepreneurship.

Alongside that professional journey, music has remained a constant part of my life. Since 2007 I have developed my own independent music projects, released two albums in Latin America and continued exploring the relationship between artistic identity, storytelling and audience development.

Today I bring those experiences together with a particular interest in music, artist development, creative strategy and projects where culture and business intersect.
