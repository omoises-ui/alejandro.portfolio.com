Artist & Artistic Project Developer · Creative Director · Journalist

Music has been part of my life since adolescence. Despite my professional journey through journalism, storytelling as a copywriter, and later creative direction, it has always remained present. I am a music lover — it may sound cliché, but it is brutally honest. That is who I am. So much so that I started creating music at a very young age, and from my teenage years to today I have been immersed in the music scenes of Caracas, Buenos Aires, Mexico and Miami, developing my own projects and helping other artists develop theirs. Today, I am immersed in the music scene of Berlin and its surrounding area, continuing to connect with artists, projects and new creative communities.

Today, I am also focused on understanding how artificial intelligence can be applied to artistic development without losing the human and creative dimension that gives art its meaning. I believe the real opportunity of new technologies lies in that balance: using them to expand possibilities, accelerate processes and support decision-making, while keeping human instinct, emotion and artistic identity at the center.
